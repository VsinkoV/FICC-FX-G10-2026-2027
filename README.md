## How to read the report

Each report opens with a **house view summary**, followed by **one page per currency**, all quoted against the US dollar and in the same layout.

- **1 to 3 month outlook:** driven by expectations. What is already priced, data surprises and positioning.
- **6 to 12 month outlook:** driven by fundamentals. Real rates, terms of trade, balance of payments, valuation and the dollar cycle.
- **Where we disagree:** where the desk's view differs from the consensus, and why.
- **Change our mind:** the specific data or events that would make the desk drop or reverse its view.
- **Trade idea:** direction from the long view and timing from the short view, with an entry, stop loss, target and position size.
- **Forecasts against the forward:** the forward is the rate you can lock in today, so a forecast only says something when it differs from the forward.
- **Indicators panel:** the key rates, data and positioning figures behind each view.
